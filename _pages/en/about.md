Based in the School of Aeronautics at Northwestern Polytechnical University, VECTOR Lab studies how aircraft reconfigure, make autonomous decisions, and maintain safe flight in complex and changing environments.

We work on reconfigurable aircraft, vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) aircraft, aerial swarms, and cross-domain uncrewed systems. Our research spans flight dynamics modeling, trajectory optimization, intelligent perception and decision-making, cooperative guidance, and flight control. We aim to make aircraft safer and more capable of completing complex missions under uncertainty.

## Research Areas

- **Intelligent flight and safe control for reconfigurable and VTOL aircraft**
  Flight dynamics modeling across configurations, configuration transitions, trajectory planning, intelligent flight control, and safety assurance.

- **Cooperative guidance and control of aerial swarms**
  Cooperative decision-making, task allocation, formation control, cooperative guidance, and autonomous swarm flight in complex environments.

- **Cooperative guidance and control of cross-domain uncrewed systems**
  Collaborative perception, planning, guidance, and control across aerial, ground, and other heterogeneous uncrewed systems.
