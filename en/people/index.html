People

Faculty, staff, and students at VECTOR Lab.

Faculty and Staff

Ban Wang

Tenured Associate Professor · PhD Supervisor

Holds a PhD in engineering from Concordia University, Canada. His research focuses on reconfigurable aircraft flight and control.

Read bio

Has led 10 national research projects, including projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China. He has published over 60 papers in journals including IEEE TIE, TAES, MSSP, and AST, and authored three monographs and two textbooks. His research covers resilient control, safe guidance, and engineering validation for reconfigurable VTOL UAVs.

Ke Zhao

Associate Research Fellow · Master's Supervisor

Holds a PhD in engineering and works in the School of Aeronautics at Northwestern Polytechnical University. His research includes aircraft conceptual design, aerodynamic design and multidisciplinary optimization, computational fluid dynamics, and active flow control.

Read bio

Serves as chief engineer on an eVTOL project and has contributed to several major national aircraft development programs. He has extensive engineering design experience and has published over 30 academic papers.

Li Tian

Lab Engineer

Works on aircraft systems design and flight control, including the development, simulation, fabrication, and flight testing of multiple rotor-wing UAVs.

Read bio

His experience includes aircraft architecture, flight-control algorithms, project coordination, and flight tests. He works with CATIA 3D modeling, flight dynamics modeling, and flight-control software in MATLAB and C/C++.
